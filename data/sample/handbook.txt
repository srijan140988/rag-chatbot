Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

1. Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried forward to the next year. Sick leave is separate and provides 10 paid days per year.

2. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 IST. Remote workers receive a one-time home-office stipend of 15000 INR.

3. Expense Reimbursement
Expenses under 5000 INR can be approved by a direct manager. Expenses above 5000 INR require finance approval. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

4. Security
All laptops must use full-disk encryption. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Report suspected phishing to security@northwind.example within one hour.
